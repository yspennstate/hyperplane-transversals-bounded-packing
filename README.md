# Hyperplane transversals of families with bounded packing number

Yitzchak Shmalo, Einstein Institute of Mathematics, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

Source and PDF of the paper of the same title.

McGinnis and Zerbib asked whether a finite family of compact convex sets in R^d with the (p,2) property is pierced
by p/d + O(1) hyperplanes. The paper shows that a finite family of compact convex sets in R^d, d >= 2, whose largest
subfamily of pairwise disjoint sets has nu members is pierced by ceil(nu/d) + 2 ceil(log2 d) + 3 hyperplanes, and
also by floor(nu/d) + 2d - 2 hyperplanes, which answers the question in the affirmative when the constant may depend
on d. Points on the moment curve show that the coefficient 1/d cannot be lowered. The additive term cannot be bounded
independently of d: hulls of the s-element subsets of points on the moment curve show that ceil(log2(nu+1))
hyperplanes are sometimes needed, and this is the exact worst case when d >= 2^(ceil(log2(nu+1)) - 1). The largest
excess over nu/d, taken over all nu, is (1+o(1)) log2 d.
