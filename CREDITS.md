# Credits

Each line: whose work, cite key, where the paper uses it.

- McGinnis and Zerbib, MZ24, Question 3.16 (quoted in Section 1) and Theorem 3.15 (the bound floor(p/2)+1 in every
  dimension, credited there to Zerbib).
- Zerbib, Zer24, Theorem 1.2 (planar bound floor(p/2)+1, tight by the regular (2p+1)-gon) in Section 1;
  Theorem 1.6 (Gallai's theorem as stated there) for T(d,n) <= n.
- Gallai, Gal59: the interval theorem behind T(d,n) <= n (Section 1).
- McGinnis, McG24: the conjecture L(k) = ceil(k/2) (its Section 4) of which the planar bound is a special
  case, as MZ24 records (Section 1). The sets of a C(k) are pairwise disjoint (McG24, after the definition), so a
  family with packing number at most p is C(p+1)-free and the conjecture gives ceil((p+1)/2) = floor(p/2)+1 lines,
  for p >= 3.
- Alon and Kalai, AK95: the (p,q) theorem for hyperplane transversals, p >= q >= d+1 (Section 1).
- Dol'nikov, Dol92: the transversal theorem, Theorem 2.2 here (Section 2), also stated as FMSS24 Theorem 1.2;
  used in Lemma 2.3 and in the proof of Theorem 1.1 (Section 4).
- Borsuk, Bor33: the Borsuk-Ulam theorem in the proof sketch of Theorem 2.2 (Section 2).
- Frick, Murray, Simon and Stemmler, FMSS24: convex hulls of large packings (Section 1 and the proof of
  Theorem 1.1); Theorem 1.5 (case m = 1) compared with Theorem 1.1 for d = 2^(k-1); Theorem 1.7 (polytopes in
  R^3 with the (8,2) property are pierced by three planes) in Section 1 and in the closing remark.
